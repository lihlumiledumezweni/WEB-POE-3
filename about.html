About Us

           

Supports gender justice and community safety, this organization exists to ensure every woman lives free from violence, discrimination, and economic vulnerability.

       
       
           

Mission and Vision

           
                   
  • Freedom from violence & bodily integrity
    •                
  • Political & Public Participation
    •                
  • Reproductive & Health Rights
    •                
  • Economic & Employment Rights
    •                
  • Legal & Civil Equality
    •                
  • Marriage & Family Rights
    •                
  • Education & Cultural Rights
    •            
       
       
           

Target Audience

           

All women and children are welcomed.

       
       
           

Goals & Objectives

           

The objective is to empower women, make all women feel safe and seen throughout without compromising who you are, what you want nor what you stand for and most importantly to live a life with living and be free.

                        

Short Term Goals (1–12 Months)

  • Community Education

  • Legal Assistance

Long Term Goals (3 to 10+ Years)

Technical Requirements

           

Hosted on Somee.com with active SSL security, registered under a dedicated .org domain (e.g., www.hlumiesngo.org.za).

           

Built with HTML5 for structure, CSS3 (using custom variables for the color palette) for styling, and JavaScript for navigation and direct social/WhatsApp links.
