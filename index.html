Brief History

The women's organization was founded in the 21st century by Lihlumile Dumezweni, who was inspired by the women in her mother's family. "I saw a gap in woman rights, education and most importantly representation which employed me to start the Hlumie's Non-Profit Organization".

Our Core Pillars

  • Political & Public Participation
  • Reproductive & Health Rights
  • Economic & Employment Rights
  • Legal & Civil Equality
  • Marriage & Family Rights
  • Education & Cultural Rights
